Dear Classmates,

It has really been hectic in a nice way. Love some, hate some — but at the end of the day, we all walked this journey together and that is what matters.

Four years of Computer Science at Kenyatta University — the late nights, the group projects, the exams, the laughter in between the chaos. We survived it all, and we are coming out stronger on the other side.

This yearbook is a little something to remember each other by. Every photo, every name, every silly label — it is all a reminder that we were part of something special. Whether we were close friends or just familiar faces in the lecture hall, we shared this chapter of our lives.

As we go our separate ways, I wish every single one of you success, happiness, and the courage to chase whatever it is you are dreaming of. Stay in touch, stay kind, and never forget where it all started.

With love,
Linet Muthoni

Behind the Scenes
